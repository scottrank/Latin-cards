How it works
  • Pick your lessons. Type the first and last lesson you want, like 1 to 5. Only those cards come up.
  • Flip, then be honest. Tap the card (or press space), then choose Got it or Missed it.
  • Miss it, see it more. A missed card comes right back in a minute or so, and cards you miss often are always shown first.
  • Know it, see it less. Each right answer pushes a card further away: 10 minutes, then 1 day, 3 days, a week, 16 days, and about a month.
  • Stubborn cards stay close. If you've missed a card 3 or more times, it won't go further than a day away until you get it right 3 times in a row.
  • Points. 10 XP for every right answer, plus a bonus of up to 10 more for a long streak. A miss earns 1 XP for trying and resets your streak. Every 250 XP is a new level.
  • Mastered means a card you've gotten right enough times that it's now a week or more away.

Trouble cards

The cards you've missed most. These come up more often until you get them right a few times in a row.
